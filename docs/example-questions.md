# Example Questions & Prompts — JustInstitutions

> Seed prompts that draw users into the system — for the landing page, per-persona entry points, Law Explorer empty states, demo scripts, and marketing. Organized by audience/area; **bold-tagged** items showcase capabilities no competitor has (judicial drift, law-as-of-date, values inference, patch methodology, DDD coherence tests, designed-system testing). Owner originals marked ★.
>
> **Living document:** as real user queries arrive, observed questions should replace invented ones.

## Patterns — what makes a question work (apply when adding new ones)

1. **A villain or a surprise beats neutrality.** "Who benefits from my state's tax carve-outs?" and "Which agencies have mandates but no budget?" pull harder than neutral queries because the answer implies accountability. "Based on our federal budget, what are our values?" is the archetype — it promises an uncomfortable mirror.
2. **Unique-capability questions are moat-demonstrators.** The bold-tagged prompts (judicial drift, law-as-of-date, values inference, J1 definition-conflicts, designed-system testing) can be answered by *no other platform* — lead with them in demos, grant proposals, and press, not just the UI.
3. **End in an artifact, not just an answer.** The staffer gets a one-page brief, the advocate a minimum-viable patch, the researcher an export. Every example prompt should lead somewhere *shareable* (stable URL) — the shared artifact is the acquisition loop.
4. **Local beats national for The People.** "My city," "my county," "my rent" outperforms abstractions; the citizen list should always parameterize on the user's jurisdiction.
5. **Skeptics are an audience too.** Questions challenging the platform ("isn't this just correlation?") are first-class prompts that route to the methodology pages — credibility through radical transparency is the brand.

## Skeptic (first-class prompts — route to methodology)

- Isn't all of this just correlation dressed up as insight? *(→ Attribution Confidence Model, C0–C3)*
- Who decided these test criteria, and why should I trust them? *(→ test-block sources: Dahl, V-Dem, WJP, Madison, Levitsky & Ziblatt…)*
- Isn't "democratic design" just a partisan lens? *(→ Universal baseline: score anyone, judge no one; Universal/Contested KPI labels)*
- How do I know the AI didn't make up these citations? *(→ citation-integrity checks; every citation resolves to source text)*
- What data is this based on, and how old is it? *(→ data vintage, corpus versioning, provenance)*
- Show me where your methodology is wrong. *(→ published rubrics; public methodology critique channel)*

## System-level / provocative (shareable headlines)

- ★ Which country has the most secure democracy?
- ★ List the 10 most significant vulnerabilities in the US system of government.
- If the US government were software, how many critical bugs would it ship with?
- Which laws produce the *opposite* of their stated intent? **(intent extraction vs. effective law)**
- What's the single highest-leverage fix in American governance — the root cause, not a symptom? **(causal chain: meta → content → outcome)**
- Which democracies are structurally one election away from breakdown?
- What's the most expensive loophole in America — and who benefits from it?
- Rank the 50 states by how well their spending matches their stated priorities. **(values inference)**
- Is the US Constitution well-architected by modern system-design standards? **(Blocks H/I/J)**
- What would the US score if judged only by its own founding intent?
- Which state governments would fail a basic engineering review?

## Engaged Citizen (issue-driven, local)

- Why is rent so high in my city — which rules are actually responsible?
- What laws govern eviction in [my county], in plain language?
- What rights do I have on paper that are practically unenforceable where I live? **(C6 paper rights)**
- Who benefits from my state's tax carve-outs?
- Which laws still on the books in my state are dead code — obsolete but never repealed?
- What would have to change — specifically — for my city to make progress on homelessness?
- How dependent is my county on federal money, and what happens in a shutdown? **(Block L fiscal dependency)**
- Is my city improving or declining over the last 20 years — and versus which peers?

## Student / Teacher (civics with real data)

- ★ In general, how are US cities governed and run?
- Show me a real example of two laws that contradict each other.
- Which state constitution is easiest for ordinary citizens to amend — and does that make it better or worse?
- Compare checks and balances: US vs. Germany vs. the UK.
- What's the oldest law still in force in my state?
- How does a bill become *effective* law — and at how many points can it die?
- Which amendment procedures make constitutions most durable?
- Why do some states have referendums and others don't?

## Journalist (outlier-hunting / story discovery)

- Which jurisdiction is the biggest outlier on maternal mortality relative to its structural peers — and what rules differ?
- Which statutes have courts effectively rewritten without any legislature voting? **(judicial drift)**
- Which agencies have legal mandates but no budget to enforce them? **(starvation capture)**
- What did [state]'s effective election law say in 2012 vs. today, with zero legislative changes? **(law-as-of-date + Layer 3)**
- Where are penalties on the books that are never enforced? **(enforcement gap)**
- Which tax expenditures cost the most per beneficiary — and who are the beneficiaries?
- Which states quietly encode religious doctrine in civil law, despite constitutional separation? **(Block K)**

## Researcher / Grad Student (citable queries)

- How many conflicting statutory definitions of "income" exist across federal benefit programs? **(J1 ubiquitous-language failure)**
- Which policy domains are "big balls of mud" — highest internal inconsistency? **(J6)**
- Is there a dose-response relationship between campaign-finance disclosure strength and corruption perception across states? **(attribution signal S3)**
- Export every privacy-law Gap finding across all 50 states.
- Which structural (Layer 1) features correlate with democratic-backsliding indicators across countries?
- Which jurisdictions are natural experiments for [policy X] — rule changed, peers didn't? **(event-sourced corpus + peer sets)**
- What is the corpus-wide rate of Ambiguity findings per policy domain?

## Policy Advocate (evidence for a cause)

- Which 3 jurisdictions already passed the reform we want — and what happened to their outcomes?
- Show that my state is an outlier among its peers on childcare outcomes, and which rule differences track it.
- Who benefits from the status quo on [issue] — and who bears the cost? **(beneficiary analysis)**
- What's the minimum viable patch for our housing gap — the smallest change with most of the benefit?

## Staffer / Government Official (actionable)

- What are the highest-severity, lowest-difficulty patches for my jurisdiction? (quick wins)
- Which of my state's mandates are unfunded? **(L1)**
- If we pass this bill, what downstream conflicts does it create? **(simulation)**
- What committees, vote thresholds, and veto points does this fix have to survive? **(passage guidance)**
- How does my city compare to its structural peers on cost per outcome?
- Executive summary: my jurisdiction's top 5 findings, one page.

## Money Explorer (values inference)

- ★ Based on our federal budget, what are our values?
- ★ Based on the state budgets, please compare the values of each state.
- Follow the dollar: where does $1 of my federal taxes actually go?
- What ratio does each state fund education vs. incarceration?
- What share of my city's budget is core mission vs. administrative overhead? **(J8 core/supporting/generic)**
- Which city gets the most outcome per dollar in [domain]?
- How is each line of my county's budget funded — taxes, fees, debt, or transfers? **(revenue pairing)**

## Law Explorer (effective law, plain language)

- What does this law actually mean in practice — not just what it says? **(effective law synthesis)**
- Now that Chevron deference is gone, what changes for the EPA rules protecting my water?
- What governs gig work / drones / AI in my state — or is it a gap?
- Which dissenting opinions are most likely to become tomorrow's law? **(dissent tracking)**

## Design (the loop-closers)

- Draft a bill to close [gap], modeled on the best-performing jurisdiction.
- Design a city charter from scratch optimized for all nine intents — then test it. **(designed/proposed system type)**
- Stress-test this ballot initiative before anyone spends political capital on it.
- What would ranked-choice voting change in my state? Run it.
- Take my jurisdiction's worst finding and produce the one-page brief I can hand to my council member.
