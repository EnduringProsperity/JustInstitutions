Block A — Separation of Powers

Five tests that ask one question: can any single branch of this government quietly take over the others' jobs?

Every rule system divides power somehow. This block probes whether the divisions actually hold — who can override whom, what happens in emergencies, and who investigates when the watchdogs themselves are accused. Grounded in Federalist No. 51; applies to any system claiming separated powers.

What this block cannot tell you

Whether officials choose to respect these boundaries (that's Layer 5 — Practice), whether courts would rule the way the text reads, or anything about blocks it doesn't cover — elections, rights, or money each have their own.

Where it applies

Runs in the Universal baseline for any system that separates powers on paper; skipped (marked n/a, never scored) where the design doesn't claim separation — a block that doesn't apply is not a failure.
